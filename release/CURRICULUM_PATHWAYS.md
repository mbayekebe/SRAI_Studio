# SRAI Curriculum Pathways

## Volume map

| Code | Volume | Notebooks | Role |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| M1 | Mathematics of SRAI | 30 | Mathematical and statistical foundations |
| M2 | Machine Learning | 35 | Classical supervised, unsupervised, causal, and operational ML |
| M3 | Deep Learning | 30 | Neural networks, sequence models, transformers, and responsible DL |
| M4 | Generative AI, LLMs, and Agents | 30 | Generative systems, RAG, tools, agents, and architecture |
| V5 | MLOps | 15 | Packaging, deployment, monitoring, and governance |
| V6 | Decision Intelligence | 15 | Decisions under uncertainty, policy, simulation, and optimization |
| V7_A | Agriculture | 15 | Agricultural statistics and applied AI |
| V8 | Enterprise AI | 15 | Enterprise architecture, security, integration, and readiness |

## Pathway A — Full professional programme

Recommended duration: 24–30 weeks.

1. M1 in sequence.
2. M2 in sequence.
3. M3, followed by M4.
4. V5 and V6.
5. Select V7_A, V8, or both as applied capstones.

Expected workload: 6–8 hours per week, including notebook execution,
reflection, and a progressively developed capstone.

## Pathway B — Applied AI practitioner

Recommended duration: 14–16 weeks.

1. M1-N01 through M1-N17 as a foundations bridge.
2. M2-N01 through M2-N20.
3. M3-N01 through M3-N15.
4. M4-N01 through M4-N16.
5. V5-N01 through V5-N15.
6. One applied volume.

## Pathway C — Executive and decision leader

Recommended duration: 6–8 weeks.

1. Selected M2 notebooks on evaluation, fairness, monitoring, and decisions.
2. Selected M4 notebooks on RAG, agents, governance, and architecture.
3. All V6 notebooks.
4. V8 notebooks on architecture, data products, access control, and readiness.

The assessment is a decision memo and architecture review rather than a coding
exam.

## Pathway D — Agricultural AI specialization

Recommended duration: 10–12 weeks.

1. M1 probability, statistics, information, and optimization notebooks.
2. M2 forecasting, causal inference, monitoring, and decision notebooks.
3. V5 operationalization essentials.
4. All V7_A notebooks.

The capstone should use an agricultural-statistics scenario with documented
data quality, uncertainty, decision rules, and operational monitoring.

## Completion rule

A learner completes a pathway when all assigned notebooks execute, required
reflections are submitted, the capstone passes the rubric, and the learner can
explain the assumptions and limitations of the resulting system.
