# SRAI Outreach Kit

## Positioning

SRAI is an executable professional curriculum connecting mathematical
foundations, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, MLOps, decision
intelligence, agricultural applications, enterprise AI, and executive AI
transformation. Its 200 notebooks
are organized as a coherent learning system and validated as one release.

## Five-part demonstration series

1. From mathematics to a defensible ML decision.
2. Building and evaluating a neural model.
3. Creating a grounded RAG and tool-using agent.
4. Deploying, monitoring, and governing the model.
5. Applying the system to agriculture or enterprise performance.

Each demonstration should last 12–20 minutes and end with a link to the
relevant learning pathway rather than attempting to cover an entire volume.

## Pilot invitation

Institutions and practitioners are invited to join a structured SRAI pilot.
Participants will follow a selected pathway, execute the assigned notebooks,
complete applied exercises, and provide feedback on clarity, relevance, and
delivery. The pilot is designed for evidence-based improvement before the
broader public release.
