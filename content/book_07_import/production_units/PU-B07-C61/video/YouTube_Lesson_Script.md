# Coordinate Support Without Scoring Human Worth — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Coordinate Support Without Scoring Human Worth** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A multidisciplinary team uses a transparent needs review to offer options and track referrals without ranking people’s value.

## Safeguard
Opaque prioritization can dehumanize people, encode discrimination, expose sensitive histories, and deny support without explanation or appeal. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
