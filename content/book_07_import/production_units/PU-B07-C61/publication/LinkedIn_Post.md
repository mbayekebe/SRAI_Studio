# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Coordinate Support Without Scoring Human Worth** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: coordinate homelessness and housing support through needs, preferences, urgency, eligibility, service capacity, consent, privacy, referral, and accountable casework—never a score of human worth.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
