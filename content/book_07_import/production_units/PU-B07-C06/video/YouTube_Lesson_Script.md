# V7_A_N01 - Operate the Agricultural Intelligence Platform — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **V7_A_N01 - Operate the Agricultural Intelligence Platform** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A national crop-monitoring platform combines synthetic field, weather, and market signals to prioritize authorized agronomic review.

## Safeguard
A platform can centralize error as easily as insight when ownership, escalation, and decision rights are unclear. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
