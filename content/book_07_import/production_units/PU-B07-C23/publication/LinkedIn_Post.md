# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Maternal and Child Health: Support, Not Scoring** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: use maternal and child health analytics to support timely human review and referral without scoring dignity, eligibility, or clinical worth.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
