# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Equity Is a Distribution, Not an Average** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: assess education equity through distributions, intersectional groups, access, participation, learning, resources, and uncertainty—not averages alone.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
