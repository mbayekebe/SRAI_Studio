# Equity Is a Distribution, Not an Average — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Equity Is a Distribution, Not an Average** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A district compares learning and access distributions across location, disability, gender, and socioeconomic groups.

## Safeguard
National or district averages can conceal severe disadvantage and direct resources away from learners facing the greatest barriers. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
