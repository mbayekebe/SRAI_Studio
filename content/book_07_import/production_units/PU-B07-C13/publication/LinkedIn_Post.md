AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Rainfall and Drought Impact Monitoring** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: link rainfall anomalies to exposure and observed agricultural impacts before escalating drought-related decisions.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
