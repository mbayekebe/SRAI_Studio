# Rainfall and Drought Impact Monitoring — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Rainfall and Drought Impact Monitoring** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A rainfall deficit is combined with crop calendars, geographic coverage, and field observations before a district is referred for review.

## Safeguard
Rainfall deficit alone does not prove crop loss, household impact, or the need for a specific intervention. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
