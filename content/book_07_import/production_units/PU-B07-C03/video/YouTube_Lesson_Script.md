# Quality Is a Decision Variable — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Quality Is a Decision Variable** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Worked example
Weighted coverage reveals when a small number of missing reporting units represent a large share of the affected population.

## Safeguard
A composite quality average can conceal a failed critical field, unlawful source, unusable delay, or severe subgroup gap. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
