# See Urban Change Without Inventing Tenure — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **See Urban Change Without Inventing Tenure** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A planning team confirms observed land-cover change with authorized records and field evidence before considering action.

## Safeguard
Remote-sensing classifications can be misused to invent tenure claims, criminalize residents, or justify displacement without due process. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
