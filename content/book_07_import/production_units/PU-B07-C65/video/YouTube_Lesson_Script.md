# National Habitat Intelligence Capstone — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **National Habitat Intelligence Capstone** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A national brief connects geographic evidence, service gaps, risks, investment scenarios, responsible owners, safeguards, and review dates.

## Safeguard
Integration without governance can centralize spatial error, expose vulnerable populations, erase local context, and amplify harmful land or housing decisions. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
