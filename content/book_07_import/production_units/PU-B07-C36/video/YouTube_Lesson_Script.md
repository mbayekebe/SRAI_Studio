# Attendance and Dropout Early-Warning Support — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Attendance and Dropout Early-Warning Support** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A school team reviews sustained absence with contextual evidence before offering support through an authorized pathway.

## Safeguard
A risk score can stigmatize learners or trigger punitive action when context, uncertainty, and human review are missing. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
