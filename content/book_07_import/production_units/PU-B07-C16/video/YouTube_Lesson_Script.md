# Agricultural Census and Frame Intelligence — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Agricultural Census and Frame Intelligence** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A farm frame separates households, holdings, parcels, and establishments while recording births, deaths, splits, merges, and coverage gaps.

## Safeguard
A frame can look complete while omitting new farms, duplicating holdings, or confusing households, parcels, and enterprises. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
