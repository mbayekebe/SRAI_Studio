AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Trust the Health Indicator Only After Testing Its Denominator** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: trust a health indicator only after testing its numerator, denominator, target population, reference period, coverage, and revision status.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
