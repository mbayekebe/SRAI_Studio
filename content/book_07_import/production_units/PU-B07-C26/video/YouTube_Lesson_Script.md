# Trust the Health Indicator Only After Testing Its Denominator — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Trust the Health Indicator Only After Testing Its Denominator** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A maternal-care rate uses eligible pregnancies rather than all women as its denominator.

## Safeguard
A plausible rate can mislead when its denominator excludes, duplicates, or misclassifies the population at risk. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
