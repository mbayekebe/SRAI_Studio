# Urban Finance and Investment Scenarios — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Urban Finance and Investment Scenarios** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A city compares infrastructure investment scenarios across service benefits, debt, maintenance, affordability, and neighbourhood equity.

## Safeguard
Opaque financial models can privilege headline returns while hiding long-term liabilities, displacement, unequal burdens, and implementation limits. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
