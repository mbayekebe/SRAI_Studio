AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Urban Finance and Investment Scenarios** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: compare urban-finance and investment scenarios using transparent assumptions, lifecycle costs, revenue, affordability, distributional effects, delivery capacity, uncertainty, and accountable public choices.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
