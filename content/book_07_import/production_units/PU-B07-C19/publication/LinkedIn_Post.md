# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Food Security Is More Than Food Supply** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: assess food security through availability, access, utilization, stability, vulnerability, and uncertainty with appropriate household and population denominators.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
