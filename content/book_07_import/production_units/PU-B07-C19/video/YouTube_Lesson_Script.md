# Food Security Is More Than Food Supply — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Food Security Is More Than Food Supply** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A food-security brief combines supply, market access, diet, coping, seasonality, and shock exposure before escalation.

## Safeguard
National food availability can improve while vulnerable households still face severe access, nutrition, or stability failures. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
