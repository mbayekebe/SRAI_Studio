# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Land Signals With Due Process** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: use land and tenure signals only to support authorized review with provenance, confidence, competing claims, privacy, notice, appeal, and due process.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
