# Medicines Before Stock-Out — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Medicines Before Stock-Out** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A vaccine alert combines usable stock, consumption, wastage, lead time, storage capacity, and expiry dates.

## Safeguard
Inventory totals can overstate usable supply when products are expired, inaccessible, misclassified, or cold-chain compromised. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
