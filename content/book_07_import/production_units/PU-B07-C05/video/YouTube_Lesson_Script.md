# From Signal to Accountable Action — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **From Signal to Accountable Action** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Worked example
Repeated alerts update one governed case with acknowledgement, escalation, resolution, and expiry times rather than creating alert fatigue.

## Safeguard
An unreviewable queue or unexplained dashboard score creates operational noise and can shift authority silently to the model. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
