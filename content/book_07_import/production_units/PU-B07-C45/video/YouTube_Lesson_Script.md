# Keep Learning Going When Hazards Arrive — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Keep Learning Going When Hazards Arrive** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A district tests heat, flood, access, and continuity scenarios before activating a proportionate school-safety plan.

## Safeguard
Hazard alerts without exposure, capacity, or authority can cause unnecessary closure, unequal disruption, or delayed protective action. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
