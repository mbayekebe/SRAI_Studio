# Seasonal Crop Production Forecasting — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Seasonal Crop Production Forecasting** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A seasonal production forecast is compared with historical and trend baselines, then withheld when interval coverage or input quality fails.

## Safeguard
A precise point forecast can mislead procurement and food-security planning when seasonality, revisions, or uncertainty are hidden. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
