# V7_A_N05 - The Agricultural Decision Room — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **V7_A_N05 - The Agricultural Decision Room** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A drought signal enters a review queue with evidence, uncertainty, owner, response capacity, escalation time, and expiry criteria.

## Safeguard
Dashboards without workflow can amplify alerts while obscuring who reviews, acts, escalates, resolves, or retires a case. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
