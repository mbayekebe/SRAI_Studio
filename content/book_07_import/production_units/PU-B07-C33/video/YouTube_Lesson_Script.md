# V7_B_N13 - Emergency Operations Decision Room — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **V7_B_N13 - Emergency Operations Decision Room** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
An incident brief links severity, resources, responsible owners, review times, and escalation paths.

## Safeguard
A fast dashboard can amplify unverified signals and obscure operational authority. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
