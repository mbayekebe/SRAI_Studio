# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Shelter Before Disaster** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: plan emergency shelter by connecting hazards, exposure, vulnerability, accessibility, capacity, routes, uncertainty, safeguarding, and authorized emergency action.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
