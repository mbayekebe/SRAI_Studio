# Shelter Before Disaster — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Shelter Before Disaster** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
An emergency team tests flood scenarios against population needs, accessible routes, shelter capacity, and backup options.

## Safeguard
A shelter count alone can fail when locations are unsafe, inaccessible, undersized, or disconnected from transport and protection services. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
