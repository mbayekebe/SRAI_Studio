# See the Service Gaps: Informal-Settlement Mapping — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **See the Service Gaps: Informal-Settlement Mapping** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
Residents and service agencies validate aggregated water, sanitation, access, drainage, and waste gaps before prioritization.

## Safeguard
High-resolution maps can expose vulnerable households, reinforce stigma, or become instruments of eviction rather than service improvement. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
