# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Anomaly Is Not Guilt** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: use examination anomalies as review signals—not proof of misconduct—while preserving evidence, uncertainty, confidentiality, due process, and authorized investigative authority.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
