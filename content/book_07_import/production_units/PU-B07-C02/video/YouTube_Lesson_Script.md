# Build the Evidence Contract — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Build the Evidence Contract** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence

Explain the principle, demonstrate the corresponding notebook evidence, and pause for one learner application.

## Worked example
A rainfall station contract binds the identifier, coordinates version, unit, observation time, calibration status, provenance, and quality owner.

## Safeguard
Successful joins can create false evidence when concepts, periods, populations, or lawful purposes are incompatible. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
