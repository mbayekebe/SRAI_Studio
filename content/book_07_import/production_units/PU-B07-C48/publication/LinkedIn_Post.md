# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Policy Simulation for Education Finance** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: use education-finance simulations to compare transparent scenarios, distributional effects, implementation constraints, uncertainty, and fiscal trade-offs—not to predict a single certain future.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
