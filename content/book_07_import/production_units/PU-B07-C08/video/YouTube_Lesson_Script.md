# Quality Before Yield — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Quality Before Yield** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
Weighted coverage shows that missing reports from two high-production districts can invalidate an otherwise high national completion rate.

## Safeguard
Yield estimates may look precise while critical production areas, seasons, or crops remain systematically under-observed. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
