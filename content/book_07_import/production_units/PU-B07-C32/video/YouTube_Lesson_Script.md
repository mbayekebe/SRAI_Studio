# V7_B_N12 - Health Surveys and Small-Area Evidence — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **V7_B_N12 - Health Surveys and Small-Area Evidence** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
District estimates combine survey design evidence with transparent uncertainty and suppression rules.

## Safeguard
Small-area precision can be overstated when design, sparsity, or model dependence is hidden. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
