# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Fix the School Data Before Judging the School** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: repair and qualify school data before using it to compare performance, allocate resources, escalate concerns, or judge institutions.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
