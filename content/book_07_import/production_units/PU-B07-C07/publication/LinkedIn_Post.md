# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Ingest Once, Preserve the Evidence** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: create a traceable agricultural evidence layer that preserves source meaning, provenance, units, time, geography, and lawful use through ingestion.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
