# Ingest Once, Preserve the Evidence — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Ingest Once, Preserve the Evidence** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A rainfall observation retains station identity, coordinate version, unit, timestamp, calibration state, and source checksum after ingestion.

## Safeguard
Convenient pipelines can erase provenance or change meaning, leaving downstream agricultural decisions impossible to audit. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
