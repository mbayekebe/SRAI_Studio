# Inclusion Begins by Seeing Barriers — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Inclusion Begins by Seeing Barriers** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A school combines accessibility review, learner and caregiver input, and service capacity before agreeing a support plan.

## Safeguard
Deficit-based classification can stigmatize learners, expose sensitive information, and substitute labels for removal of institutional barriers. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
