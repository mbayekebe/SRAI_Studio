# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Inclusion Begins by Seeing Barriers** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: identify and remove barriers to education using accessible evidence, lived experience, reasonable accommodation, referral pathways, and accountable human review.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
