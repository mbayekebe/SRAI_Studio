# Earlier Signals, Safer Escalation — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Earlier Signals, Safer Escalation** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A persistent multi-source risk signal triggers a time-bound analyst review rather than an automatic food-security classification.

## Safeguard
Early-warning systems can generate harmful false alarms or dangerous silence when thresholds and escalation duties are unclear. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
