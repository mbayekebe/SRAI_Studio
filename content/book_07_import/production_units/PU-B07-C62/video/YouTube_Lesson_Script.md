# Census, Address, and Geospatial Frames — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Census, Address, and Geospatial Frames** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A statistical office reconciles address, building, imagery, and enumeration evidence before updating a census frame.

## Safeguard
Incomplete or duplicated frames can omit populations, distort allocation, expose households, and create false confidence in geographic coverage. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
