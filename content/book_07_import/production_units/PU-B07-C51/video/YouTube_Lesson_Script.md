# Housing Need Beyond Counts — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Housing Need Beyond Counts** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A city compares household formation, rents, incomes, vacancies, housing quality, and location before defining an intervention.

## Safeguard
Unit counts can conceal unaffordability, overcrowding, poor quality, exclusion, and housing that is disconnected from livelihoods and services. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
