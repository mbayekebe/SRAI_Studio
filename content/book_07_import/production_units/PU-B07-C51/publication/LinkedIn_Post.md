# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Housing Need Beyond Counts** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: assess housing need through households, adequacy, affordability, location, tenure context, supply, displacement risk, and uncertainty—not dwelling counts alone.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
