# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Outbreak Signals Before Alarm** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: detect possible outbreaks through governed baselines, thresholds, persistence, corroboration, uncertainty, and authorized public-health escalation.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
