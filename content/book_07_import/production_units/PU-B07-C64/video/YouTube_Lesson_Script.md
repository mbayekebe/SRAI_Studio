# Evaluate Place-Based Programmes — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Evaluate Place-Based Programmes** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
An evaluation tests whether neighbourhood improvement reflects programme contribution, wider trends, displacement, or changes in who lives there.

## Safeguard
Simple before-and-after area comparisons can mistake population change or spillovers for programme impact and conceal who lost. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
