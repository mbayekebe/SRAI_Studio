AI projects often begin with data or a model. **One Service Picture, Three Different Systems** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: create a coherent wash service picture by reconciling infrastructure, service delivery, household experience, quality, reliability, accessibility, and institutional responsibility.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
