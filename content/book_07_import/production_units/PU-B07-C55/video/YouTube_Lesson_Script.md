# One Service Picture, Three Different Systems — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **One Service Picture, Three Different Systems** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A municipality compares network records, provider operations, and household evidence before declaring an area served.

## Safeguard
Infrastructure presence can be mistaken for reliable service when water is unsafe, intermittent, unaffordable, inaccessible, or poorly maintained. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
