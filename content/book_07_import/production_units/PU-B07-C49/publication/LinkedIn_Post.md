# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Evaluate Programmes, Not Just Scores** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: evaluate education programmes using explicit theory, counterfactual logic, implementation evidence, outcomes, equity, costs, uncertainty, and decision relevance.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
