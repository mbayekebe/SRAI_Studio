# Evaluate Programmes, Not Just Scores — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Evaluate Programmes, Not Just Scores** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
An evaluation separates score change from plausible programme contribution and checks who benefited, at what cost, and under which conditions.

## Safeguard
Before-and-after scores can misattribute change and encourage scaling a programme that was ineffective, unequal, or poorly implemented. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
