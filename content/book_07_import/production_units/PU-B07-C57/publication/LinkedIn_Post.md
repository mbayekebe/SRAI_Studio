# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Access Means Reaching Opportunity Reliably** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: measure mobility by reliable access to opportunities across time, cost, safety, disability, gender, geography, service variability, and uncertainty—not distance alone.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
