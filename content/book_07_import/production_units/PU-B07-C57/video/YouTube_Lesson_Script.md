# Access Means Reaching Opportunity Reliably — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Access Means Reaching Opportunity Reliably** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A city compares actual travel reliability and affordability to jobs, schools, health care, and markets across neighbourhoods.

## Safeguard
Average travel time can hide unreliable service, inaccessible routes, unsafe transfers, and unequal opportunity. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
