# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Education Surveys and Frames** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: design education surveys with explicit populations, frames, selection probabilities, weights, nonresponse treatment, precision, disclosure controls, and fitness for the intended decision.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
