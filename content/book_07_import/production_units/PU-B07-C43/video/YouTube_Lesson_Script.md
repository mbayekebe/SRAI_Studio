# From School to Work Without Chasing Yesterday — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **From School to Work Without Chasing Yesterday** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A transition review combines graduate outcomes, employer evidence, occupation change, and programme capacity before recommending adaptation.

## Safeguard
Training against yesterday’s vacancies can lock learners into obsolete pathways and overstate the certainty of labour-market forecasts. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
