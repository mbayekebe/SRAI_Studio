# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **From School to Work Without Chasing Yesterday** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: align school-to-work intelligence with changing labour demand, transferable skills, transition evidence, uncertainty, and accountable education and labour institutions.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
