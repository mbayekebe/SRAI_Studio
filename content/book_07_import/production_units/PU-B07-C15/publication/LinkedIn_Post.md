AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Satellites Need Ground Truth** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: integrate remote-sensing evidence only after documenting spatial resolution, temporal alignment, cloud and sensor limits, ground truth, and uncertainty.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
