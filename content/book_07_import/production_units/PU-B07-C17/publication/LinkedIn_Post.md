# LinkedIn publication draft

AI projects often begin with data or a model. **Samples That Can Represent a Nation** begins with the institutional decision.

The practical question is: design agricultural samples whose target population, frame, stratification, selection probabilities, weights, and precision support national decisions.

The companion lesson and executable notebook show how to make that requirement testable while keeping uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and accountable human authority visible.

This is synthetic instructional material—not an operational decision system.
