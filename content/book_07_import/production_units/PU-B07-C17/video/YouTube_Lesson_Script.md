# Samples That Can Represent a Nation — Video Lesson Script

## Opening
What decision are we trying to improve, who owns it, and what must remain under human authority? This lesson uses **Samples That Can Represent a Nation** to make that question operational.

## Learning promise
By the end, learners can explain the core contract, test it using the notebook, identify failure conditions, and state when the system must abstain.

## Teaching sequence


## Worked example
A stratified sample preserves selection probabilities and calculates weights, nonresponse adjustments, and design-based precision.

## Safeguard
A large sample is not nationally representative when coverage, selection probabilities, or weights are defective. The model supports review; the accountable institution retains authorization and action.

## Closing challenge
Ask learners to rewrite the example for another sector while preserving evidence, uncertainty, abstention, escalation, and human authority.
